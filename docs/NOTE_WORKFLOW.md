# Daily note Draft Workflow

The note workflow should turn a full day of research into one useful, readable article draft.

It is not a dump of every hourly experiment.

## Goal

Target human workload:

> Open the prepared draft once per day, review it, make any desired edit, and press Publish.

## Daily selection

At the end of the day, rank experiments by:

1. educational value
2. clarity of before/after or hypothesis/result story
3. visual strength
4. novelty
5. relevance to people using GPT image generation
6. social response when available

Social response is supporting evidence, not the only selection criterion.

## Recommended article structure

### 1. Hook

Start with the question or surprising result.

Example pattern:

> “Cinematic lighting”より、光の役割を分けて指定した方が本当に良くなるのか？

### 2. Today's experiment

Explain:

- what was being tested
- why it mattered
- what changed from the previous attempt

### 3. Visual result

Show the strongest relevant image, comparison, or failure.

### 4. Observation

Explain concrete differences rather than vague quality claims.

### 5. What was learned

Give the reader at least one useful principle for free.

### 6. Prompt insight

Reveal the relevant idea or partial prompt structure when appropriate.

Do not rely on artificial withholding. The public content should be genuinely useful on its own.

### 7. What remains uncertain

Research credibility improves when uncertainty is visible.

### 8. Next experiment

Show that the lab is continuing rather than presenting every result as final truth.

### 9. CTA

Possible CTA directions:

- follow the ongoing experiment series
- invite readers to suggest a difficult image challenge
- point to a future consolidated Prompt OS / methodology
- offer a deeper prompt breakdown when an appropriate follower / product flow exists

CTA wording should follow note/platform rules and avoid misleading promises.

## Draft package

The daily automation should prepare:

- article title
- optional subtitle / lead
- hero image selection
- full article body
- inline image placement plan
- experiment references
- partial prompt / principle to reveal
- CTA
- tags
- optional social teaser copy

## Human gate

Keep final publication human-controlled during the initial system.

Reasons:

- catches broken formatting or accidental claims
- prevents low-quality daily research from becoming public automatically
- preserves editorial judgment
- reduces platform automation risk

## Quality gate

Do not create a forced article if the day's research produced no meaningful story.

Preferred fallback:

- combine two days
- publish a short failure report when genuinely informative
- skip rather than manufacture a weak conclusion

## Product funnel principle

The long-term product should not be positioned as merely a collection of hidden prompts.

The stronger product is the accumulated methodology:

- how to diagnose an image
- how to choose the next change
- how to structure prompts by visual function
- how to detect model failure
- how to improve repeatedly
- what principles survive multiple experiments

The note should gradually demonstrate that capability in public.
