# Candidate Learnings

This file is the staging area between raw experiment notes and the durable `PROMPT_PLAYBOOK.md`.

A candidate learning may be useful without being proven.

## Promotion rule

Promote a candidate into `PROMPT_PLAYBOOK.md` only when at least one of the following is true:

1. multiple experiments support it across similar conditions
2. a controlled comparison shows a clear and interpretable effect
3. it survives a deliberate replication attempt

Before promotion, record scope and limitations.
